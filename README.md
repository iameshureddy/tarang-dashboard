# Tarang Go-To-Market Dashboard — Furniture Segment

A business intelligence and market expansion analytics project built to help **Tarang Smart Furniture** evaluate furniture retailers, prioritize high-potential stores, and make data-driven go-to-market decisions using SQL, Microsoft Excel, DAX, and Power BI.

## Project Overview

Expanding into new retail markets requires identifying the right stores, understanding local opportunities, and allocating sales visits effectively. This project transforms furniture-store data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that supports store prioritization, area-level market analysis, and sales execution planning.

The dashboard provides insights into high-priority stores, target outlets, premium retailers, multi-brand opportunities, shop ratings, and area-level expansion potential.

## Business Objectives

- Identify high-potential furniture retailers for market expansion.
- Rank and prioritize stores based on business criteria.
- Analyze area-level opportunities to support go-to-market planning.
- Identify premium, highly rated, and multi-brand retailers.
- Improve sales-visit prioritization and resource allocation.
- Present actionable business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Technology Stack

- **SQL:** Data extraction, querying, and preparation.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection, organization, and preliminary analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, business reporting, and visualization.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations, measures, and analytical logic.
- **Data Analytics:** Store ranking, segmentation, opportunity analysis, and business interpretation.

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Organize the furniture-store dataset and prepare the fields required for analysis.
2. **Data Analysis:** Use SQL and Excel to examine store attributes, business categories, and relevant performance indicators.
3. **KPI Development:** Apply DAX to calculate and display business metrics.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive Power BI visuals for store rankings, market areas, and target criteria.
5. **Business Interpretation:** Translate analytical findings into actionable store-prioritization and market-expansion insights.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard's analytical focus:

- Prioritize high-scoring stores for sales outreach.
- Focus expansion efforts on areas with stronger identified opportunities.
- Evaluate premium and multi-brand retailers for potential strategic partnerships.
- Use store rankings and target criteria to organize sales visits.
- Review dashboard filters and KPIs regularly as the underlying business data changes.

## Business Impact

The dashboard provides a structured approach to retail market assessment by consolidating store-level and area-level insights into a single interactive reporting interface. It supports more focused sales outreach, clearer prioritization, and data-informed go-to-market planning.
